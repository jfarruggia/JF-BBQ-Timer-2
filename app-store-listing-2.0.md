## App Review Information — Notes

Thank you for reviewing Grill Time Pro 2.0.

What's new: an Apple Watch companion app, support for the Combustion wireless meat thermometer (Bluetooth), an optional Total Time alert, and a visual redesign.

Optional hardware. The Combustion probe is third-party Bluetooth hardware. The app works fully without it. Tapping the "Probe" button in the header opens a scanner; with no probe nearby it simply shows an empty list. Bluetooth is used only to read temperatures from that probe (see NSBluetoothAlwaysUsageDescription). No hardware is needed to review the app.

Premium (one-time purchase, $3.99). The in-app purchase "Premium Features Unlock" (com.jamesfarruggia.JF_BBQ_Timer.premium.lifetime, non-consumable) unlocks: the Apple Watch app, the probe feature, more timers (up to 10), premium alert sounds, and custom sounds. Free users get 2 timers with full flip alerts. The paywall opens from the gold "Upgrade to Premium" row at the top of Settings, from onboarding screen 3, or from the "Probe" button in the header. It can be tested with a sandbox account. "Restore Purchases" is on the paywall.

Apple Watch. The Watch app requires the paired iPhone app and a Premium unlock. Without Premium, the Watch shows a single screen asking the user to unlock on the iPhone. With Premium, it mirrors the iPhone's timers and lets the user start, flip, and reset them.

Notifications. Local notifications are used for timer alerts (time to flip, cook done, probe target reached). No push notifications, no accounts, no sign-in.

No account is required. There is no login.

Contact for review questions: jfarruggia@farruggiacreations.com
